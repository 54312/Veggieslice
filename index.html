Privacy Policy for Veggie Slice

Developer: Sabir Murshed Ansari  |  Effective Date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how personal information and device data are collected, used, and shared when you install or use the mobile application Veggie Slice ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

Veggie Slice is designed to respect your privacy. The App itself does not require user registration or personal account creation. However, third-party services integrated into the App may collect certain information to function properly:

  • Advertising ID & Device Identifiers: We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process your mobile device's Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, system information, and performance data to serve relevant, personalized, or non-personalized ads.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: We use analytics and diagnostic tools to collect pseudonymous information regarding app performance, usage statistics, and crash logs to improve app stability and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data collected via integrated SDKs (such as Google AdMob and Google Play Services) is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To display advertisements within the application.
  • To monitor, analyze, and optimize app performance and stability.
  • To prevent fraud and ensure policy compliance.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information on how they handle data:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

4. Data Retention & Security

We do not operate external servers that store your personal identifier data directly. Any data collected by third-party advertising or analytics networks is handled in accordance with their strict security standard practices and retention policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Veggie Slice does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
